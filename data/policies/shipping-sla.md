# Shipping Service Levels

## Targets

| Stage | Standard | Expedited ($25) |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch from warehouse | within 2 business days | same business day |
| Delivery after dispatch | 5 business days | 2 business days |

An order still within its dispatch or delivery target is **on time**. Reassure the customer,
restate the expected date, and take no further action.

## Tracking staleness

Staleness is measured from the carrier's last scan.

- **Under 7 days** — normal. Transit gaps of a few days are expected on long routes.
  Reply only.
- **7 to 13 days** — stalled. **Resend the tracking link** to the customer, confirm the
  carrier and tracking number, and tell them we are monitoring it.
- **14 days or more** — treat the parcel as **lost in transit**. Send a replacement unit at
  our cost and open a carrier claim internally. Do not ask the customer to wait longer, and
  do not make them chase the carrier themselves.

## No scan yet

A shipment created but never scanned is normal for the first 2 business days. After that,
treat it as stalled and resend tracking.

## Marked delivered but not received

If tracking shows delivered and the customer says nothing arrived, **escalate to a human
agent**. These require a carrier investigation and a signed statement, which an automated
reply cannot produce. Do not auto-refund and do not auto-replace.

## Expedited shipping that misses its target

When an expedited order misses the 2-business-day delivery target, refund the **$25 expedited
fee** as a partial refund and apologise plainly. The rest of the order stands. See the refund
policy for the partial-refund rule.

## Address changes

An address change requested **before dispatch** can be actioned directly. After dispatch it
requires a carrier intercept, so **escalate to a human agent**. Never promise an intercept
will succeed.

## Carrier contacts

Northline, Veloce and Postaro all expose public tracking pages. Always give the customer the
carrier name together with the tracking number — a bare number is not useful to them.
