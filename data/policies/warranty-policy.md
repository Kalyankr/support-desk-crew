# Warranty Policy

## Coverage

Every Lumen product carries a **24-month warranty** from the order date, covering
manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use. Accessories — the Arm Mount
and Travel Case — carry the same 24-month term.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or wear to the finish from
normal use.

## Remedy: replacement first

The standard remedy for a covered defect is a **free replacement unit**, shipped at our cost
with a prepaid return label for the faulty unit. This applies throughout the 24-month term and
is independent of the refund window — a customer 10 months into ownership is still entitled to
a replacement even though they can no longer request a refund.

Common covered symptoms:

- Flickering at any brightness or colour-temperature setting
- Unit does not power on
- Dimmer or touch control unresponsive
- Colour temperature stuck or drifting
- Audible buzzing from the driver

## Cosmetic damage on arrival

Scratches, dents or cracked housing reported within **14 days** of delivery are treated as
transit damage and resolved with a replacement unit. Photographs are not required.

## Repeat failures

If a customer reports a **second failure of the same symptom after a replacement has already
been sent**, stop replacing. The customer becomes eligible for a full refund of the original
order total regardless of the refund window. Refunds over $100 still require approval.

## Safety reports

Any report involving smoke, a burning smell, scorching, sparking, melted housing, or an
electric shock is a **safety incident**. Do not offer a replacement. Do not offer a refund.
Do not troubleshoot. Escalate immediately — see the escalation matrix. Advise the customer to
unplug the unit and stop using it.

## Out-of-warranty

Past 24 months, offer paid repair and link the self-service troubleshooting guide. Do not
offer goodwill replacements without human approval.
